## About Me 

I am a **Ph.D. student** at Stanford University, advised by Professor [Daniel McFarland](https://profiles.stanford.edu/daniel-mcfarland) in the [MIMIR Knowledge Creation Lab](https://mimir.stanford.edu). I have also had the pleasure of learning from and collaborating with Professors Victor R. Lee, Melissa Valentine, Julian Nyarko, and Diyi Yang.

## Research Interests

I am a **computational social scientist**. My research focuses on how institutions evaluate knowledge and innovations. My methods include natural language processing, social network analysis, causal inference, and LLM-based simulation.

I am particularly interested in: 

- Evaluation in science and innovation: how peer review and patent examination shape which ideas advance in the knowledge economy, and how targeted AI augmentation can improve these systems
- Evaluating AI in expert domains: how to assess the quality of AI-generated work and judgments in high-stakes settings such as law and peer review, and when rubrics, expert preferences, or LLM judges can be trusted
- AI in Education: how students and teachers adopt generative AI in secondary schools, and what this means for academic integrity and how student work is assessed
